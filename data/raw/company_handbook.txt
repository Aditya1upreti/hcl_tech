HCLTech Career Shaper Programme
Welcome to the HCLTech Career Shaper Programme! This handbook provides an overview of the programme structure, resources, and support available to all participants.
PROGRAMME STRUCTURE
The Career Shaper Programme is a comprehensive 6-month training and development programme designed to equip participants with practical skills in technology and professional practice. The programme combines theoretical learning, hands-on projects, and industry exposure.
PROGRAMME DURATION
- Total Duration: 6 months
- Phase 1: Foundation (Month 1-2): Core concepts, foundational skills, and assessment
- Phase 2: Specialization (Month 3-4): Track-specific skills and industry projects
- Phase 3: Capstone (Month 5-6): Final project presentation and career support
PROGRAMME TRACKS
- Software Development
- Data Science and Analytics
- Cloud and Infrastructure
- Cybersecurity
- Full Stack Development
KEY DATES
- Orientation: First Monday of each cohort cycle
- Mid-term Review: End of Month 3
- Capstone Project Submission: Last week of Month 5
- Graduation: Last day of Month 6
SUPPORT RESOURCES
- Programme Coordinators: Available for academic and administrative support
- Learning Management System: Access to course materials, assignments, and grades
- Career Services: Resume building, interview preparation, and job placement assistance
- Student Support: Personal and academic counselling services
CODE OF CONDUCT
All participants are expected to adhere to the Code of Conduct, which outlines the standards of behaviour expected during the programme. A separate Code of Conduct document is provided.
POLICY ACCESS
All programme policies (Security, Privacy, Leave, Expense, WFH, etc.) are available on the participant portal or upon request from the programme office.
